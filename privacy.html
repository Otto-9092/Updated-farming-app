Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

OπO Farming (the "App") is a precision-agriculture Progressive Web App operated by Diamond O Farms LLC, a Nebraska-based family farming operation. This policy describes what information the App collects, how it is used, where it is stored, and how it is shared.

This App is operated for the private use of Diamond O Farms LLC. We do not sell data, do not run advertising, and do not share your information with any third party except as described below.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy covers two groups of people:

  • Operators — the farmer(s) running the App in-cab on a phone or tablet to record field operations.
  • Customers — individuals or operations that receive a PDF map export (As-Planted, As-Harvested, or As-Applied) from an Operator after work has been performed on their field.

2. What information the App collects

From Operators

  • GPS location data while a session is active, used to paint coverage, calculate acres, and record a trail. Location is read from the device's own browser geolocation API; it is not sent to any server owned by Diamond O Farms LLC or to any third party.
  • Equipment, field, and operation details that the operator enters manually (field name, boundary, crop, variety, rate, yield, notes).
  • Operator name if entered for PDF signature lines (stored locally only).
  • Photos of seed tags and field notes if the operator chooses to capture them (stored locally in device storage; not uploaded anywhere).
  • Google account identifier if the operator signs in to enable cross-device sync (see Section 4).

From Customers

The App does not collect any data from Customers directly. Customers interact with the App only indirectly by receiving a PDF map export from an Operator. The data on that PDF is limited to what the Operator entered for the operation performed on the Customer's field:

  • Field name (as entered by the Operator)
  • Farm/Customer name (as entered by the Operator)
  • Field boundary coordinates
  • Area covered (acres)
  • Date of operation
  • Crop, variety, rate, yield (as applicable)
  • A map image of the painted coverage

3. Where data is stored

All App data is stored locally on the Operator's device, in the browser's own storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). The data does not leave the device unless the Operator takes one of these actions:

  • Enables Google Drive sync. If the Operator signs in with a Google account and uses "Sync Now", a single JSON file is written to the Operator's own Google Drive, in the Drive Application Data folder — a hidden folder that only this App can read or write, and that Diamond O Farms LLC cannot access. The data in that file is used solely to synchronize the Operator's own records between their own devices.
  • Exports a PDF, CSV, shapefile, or JSON backup. These files are produced locally on the device. The Operator is responsible for sharing or storing them.
  • Delivers a PDF to a Customer. The PDF is a file the Operator hands or emails to the Customer. Diamond O Farms LLC does not retain a copy of the PDF outside of the Operator's own records.

4. Use of Google APIs

The App uses the following Google APIs:

  • Google Maps JavaScript API and Google Static Maps API — to display satellite imagery under the live coverage map and to render the map image embedded in Customer PDFs. Map rendering requests are sent directly from the Operator's device to Google.
  • Google Identity Services (OAuth 2.0) — to let the Operator sign in with a Google account for Drive sync.
  • Google Drive API, specifically the drive.appdata scope — to read and write a single sync file in the Operator's own hidden Application Data folder. This scope does not grant access to any other file in the Operator's Drive, and the sync file is not visible or accessible to any other app or user.

OπO Farming's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. What we do with Customer data

Diamond O Farms LLC uses Customer field information (name, boundary, operation details) solely to:

  • Perform the agricultural operation the Customer has engaged us for.
  • Produce As-Planted, As-Harvested, or As-Applied PDF reports for the Customer's own records.
  • Maintain internal farm records for business, tax, and compliance purposes.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share Customer data with any third party except where legally required (e.g., crop insurance claims, FSA reporting initiated by the Customer).

6. Data retention

Operator and Customer data remains on the Operator's device until the Operator deletes it or clears browser storage. Google Drive sync files remain in the Operator's Drive until the Operator deletes them from Drive. Diamond O Farms LLC retains internal paper and digital records of operations performed for Customers in accordance with standard business recordkeeping practice (typically 7 years for tax purposes).

7. Children's privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App evolves or as our business changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.
