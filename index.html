☁️ Sync across devices (easiest):
1. Sign in with Google on each device (same account)
2. Tap 🔄 Sync Now — it merges equipment, fields & reports both ways
3. If the same item differs on two devices, you'll be asked which to keep
Note: photos stay on each device — they still travel inside any PDF you share.

📁 Manual backup (offline / no Google):
Use Export All Data to save a file, then Import Data on the other device (choose Merge to add, or Replace to overwrite).
